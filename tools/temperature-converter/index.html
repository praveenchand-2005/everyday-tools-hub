Temperature Converter

Convert temperatures between Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin, including a check for values below absolute zero.

About this tool

Celsius and Fahrenheit are common everyday scales, while Kelvin is used in science. The converter rejects temperatures below absolute zero, the physical lower limit for thermodynamic temperature.

How to use the temperature converter

  1. Enter your values in the calculator above.
  2. Choose the relevant calculation or unit options.
  3. Select the calculate or convert button to view the result.
  4. Review the result and keep any relevant context in mind before using it.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply Celsius by 9/5 and add 32.

What is absolute zero?

Absolute zero is 0 kelvin, equivalent to −273.15°C or −459.67°F.
