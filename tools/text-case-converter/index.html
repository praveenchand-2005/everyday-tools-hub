Text Case Converter

Quickly change capitalization without retyping your text. Paste text, choose a case style and copy the result.

About this tool

Case conversion is useful for headings, drafts, labels and formatting pasted text. Review names, acronyms and language-specific capitalization after converting, because automatic rules cannot reliably infer every proper noun or editorial style.

How to use the text case converter

  1. Enter your values in the calculator above.
  2. Choose the relevant calculation or unit options.
  3. Select the calculate or convert button to view the result.
  4. Review the result and keep any relevant context in mind before using it.

Frequently asked questions

What is title case?

Title case capitalizes the first letter of most words; exact style rules differ by publication.

Does the converter upload my text?

No. Conversion is performed in your browser in this static tool.
