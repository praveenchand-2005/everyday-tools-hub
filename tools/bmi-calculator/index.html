BMI Calculator

Calculate an estimated body mass index (BMI) from your height and weight. This tool is for general information, not diagnosis.

About this tool

For adults, BMI is calculated as weight (kg) divided by height (m) squared. Standard adult categories are broad screening ranges and may not apply to children, pregnancy, athletes or every individual. Do not use a BMI result as a substitute for medical advice.

How to use the bmi calculator

  1. Enter your values in the calculator above.
  2. Choose the relevant calculation or unit options.
  3. Select the calculate or convert button to view the result.
  4. Review the result and keep any relevant context in mind before using it.

Frequently asked questions

How is BMI calculated?

BMI is weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared.

Is BMI a diagnosis?

No. BMI is a screening measure and does not directly measure body fat or overall health. Ask a qualified healthcare professional about personal concerns.
