Tip & Bill Splitter

Work out a tip, the total amount and how much each person pays when splitting a bill evenly.

About this tool

This calculator is designed for an equal split. It does not account for tax rules, service charges already included on a bill, different orders or currency-specific rounding. Confirm the restaurant's bill details before paying.

How to use the tip & bill splitter

  1. Enter your values in the calculator above.
  2. Choose the relevant calculation or unit options.
  3. Select the calculate or convert button to view the result.
  4. Review the result and keep any relevant context in mind before using it.

Frequently asked questions

How is the tip calculated?

The tip is the bill amount multiplied by the tip percentage divided by 100.

Can the tool handle multiple people?

Yes. Enter a whole number of people and the total including tip is divided equally.
